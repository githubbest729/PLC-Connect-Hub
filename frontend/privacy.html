Privacy

PLC-Connect Hub stores snippets, tasks and BOM data only in your browser (IndexedDB) on this device. One localStorage flag remembers whether you finished or skipped the product tour. No account is used. The optional Live Data tab talks only to the backend you run yourself; nothing is sent to third parties.

The app loads the Tailwind CSS script from cdn.tailwindcss.com, which may log your IP address as a normal web request. After the first visit it is served from the offline cache.
